🔒 Hardware Vendor Lock-in (ACR1581U / PC-SC) Locking: PWD/PACK + AUTH0=04 + PROT=1
This step prepares blank NTAG213/215/216 chips prior to delivery. It programs the FOXY magic bytes, merchant network hash, digital HMAC signature, and activates hardware password protection. Un-provisioned or foreign cards will be rejected by merchant POS terminals.
